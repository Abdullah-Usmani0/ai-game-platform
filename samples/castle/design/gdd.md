## Concept
A chapel cut into a sea cliff, its halls crusted white with salt and its dead restless. A pilgrim in dented armour wakes below the nave with nothing but a jump. The way up is a step of stone too tall to climb, so the pilgrim turns back into the dark behind the first hall and finds a pair of wings. With the double jump the step falls; past the cloister wait the boots, and a shaft at the chapel's far end drops to the crypt below. The way back runs under the halls above, across a chasm of spikes that only a double jump and a dash can cross, to the door out beneath the first altar.

## Pillars
1. Every hall hides a way back: each wall the pilgrim cannot pass yet is seen before the relic that beats it, and the wings lie behind the start.
2. A relic reopens the castle: the wings turn the tall step into a path, and the boots turn the chasm into a bridge.
3. Rest is earned at the altar: two altars refill the pilgrim and wake the dead again; a fall returns the pilgrim to the last one, with everything found kept.

## Core loop
30 seconds: walk the first hall, meet the tall step, turn back for the wings. 5 minutes: climb, stomp the ghouls of the cloister, take the boots, drop down the shaft to the second altar, pass the hounds of the crypt and cross the chasm. Session: a few runs chasing a crossing with no falls.

## Progression
The wings, then the first altar and the tall step, then the boots, the shaft down and the second altar, then the chasm on the way back. Abilities last through falls and rests; no stretch between altars runs past four rooms.

## Scope
1 region of 13 rooms on two floors, 3 enemy types (ghoul, hound, gargoyle), 3 item types (wings, boots, souls), no bosses.
