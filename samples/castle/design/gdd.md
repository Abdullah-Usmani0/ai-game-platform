## Concept
A chapel cut into a sea cliff, its halls crusted white with salt and its dead restless. A pilgrim in dented armour wakes below the nave with nothing but a jump. The way up is a step of stone too tall to climb, so the pilgrim turns back into the dark behind the first hall and finds a pair of wings. With the double jump the step falls; past the cloister wait the boots, and beyond the crypt a chasm of spikes that only a double jump and a dash can cross. The chapel's door opens on the cliff path out.

## Pillars
1. Every hall hides a way back: each wall the pilgrim cannot pass yet is seen before the relic that beats it, and the wings lie behind the start.
2. A relic reopens the castle: the wings turn the tall step into a path, and the boots turn the chasm into a bridge.
3. Rest is earned at the altar: two altars refill the pilgrim and wake the dead again; a fall returns the pilgrim to the last one, with everything found kept.

## Core loop
30 seconds: walk the first hall, meet the tall step, turn back for the wings. 5 minutes: climb, stomp the ghouls of the cloister, take the boots, rest at the second altar, descend past the hounds and cross the chasm. Session: a few runs chasing a crossing with no falls.

## Progression
The wings, then the first altar and the tall step, then the boots and the second altar, then the descent and the chasm. Abilities last through falls and rests; no stretch between altars runs past four rooms.

## Scope
1 region of 13 rooms, 3 enemy types (ghoul, hound, gargoyle), 3 item types (wings, boots, souls), no bosses.
