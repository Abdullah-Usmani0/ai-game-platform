# Top-down action GDD fragment

Verbs: move (eight directions), dash (a short burst with a real cooldown; with dashInvuln on, the player cannot be hurt while it lasts, so a well-timed dash goes through an attack), attack (directional melee on the action key in a three-hit chain: the third swing is a finisher that hits harder, reaches further and throws foes back; a swing during or right after a dash is a lunging dash-strike; swings parry arrows and embers).
Level grammar: rooms. A floor is a grid of rectangular rooms (12-15 x 8-10 tiles of 8 px) joined by three-wide doors; the entry room is empty, the exit sits in the deepest room. Cover tiles appear from difficulty 0.35. A boss floor (generator param boss: 1) makes the deepest room a dead-end lair holding the boss above the exit.
Enemies: grunt (rushes on sight, telegraphed bite, 2 hp), archer (keeps distance, shoots arrows, 2 hp), brute (slow, long wind-up, 2-damage bite, 6 hp), warden (boss, 30 hp: slams up close after a long wind-up, fires ember rings at range, enrages at half health; never staggers). Enemies wake on sight (player_in_sight: never through a wall). Nothing hurts by touch: every attack is telegraphed (custom:windup, red flash) and a hit interrupts the small ones. Behaviour is data (BehaviourFsm) using the genre vocabulary.
Win by reaching the exit after every hostile on the floor is dead (room-clear locks it); dying ends the run. Boons (optional system): clearing a floor offers a choice of three (more damage, reach, attack speed, dash cooldown, knockback, hearts, healing); the pick carries into the next floor, so the run grows stronger. No boon after a boss floor.
Feel presets: room-melee (default), room-heavy. Feedback table must bind: hit, death, pickup, level_complete, dash; bind custom:finisher (heavy hitstop and shake) and, with a boss, custom:slam and custom:enrage.
Scope tiers: small = 2 floors, 2 enemy types; medium = 4 floors, 3 types, the last floor a boss lair; large = 6 floors, 4 types and a boss.
Ask (one at a time, short): How many floors should a run have, and should it end with a boss? Should fights be about crowds of grunts or careful duels with archers behind cover? How heavy should hits feel (light and fast, or slow and crunchy)? Should dashing be the main defence or a rare escape? What is the mood (mythic, grim, cartoon)?
