# Cozy farm GDD fragment

Verbs: move (eight directions, an unhurried walk; no dash unless a game asks for one), work (the action key uses the tool the tile in front calls for: till untilled soil, sow the seed in hand on tilled soil, water a planting once a day, harvest a ripe crop into the bag; facing the shipping bin it ships the bag, facing the stall it opens the seed shop, facing the door it asks to sleep), choose seed (jump changes the seed in hand).
Level grammar: farm. A level is one season on a fenced farm of 40 x 20 tiles of 8 px, one screen: the farmhouse at the top left with its door (the bed), the shipping bin against the house, the seed stall on the east fence, a field of soil plots in the middle (8 at the easiest season, up to 30, in two fields from difficulty 0.55), trees and bushes around. The player wakes on the door step every morning.
The day: 6 AM to 2 AM over dayLengthS seconds (default 120). Sleeping ends the day early; at 2 AM the farmer falls asleep where it stands and wakes on half its energy. Overnight, watered plantings grow a day, the soil dries, and the bin pays for what was shipped; a card says how much the next morning.
Economy: content/crops.json (optional) lists up to 8 crops with a seed price, a sell price, the days to ripen, a regrowth for crops that bear again, and a look (root, leafy, tall or bush, and a colour); the default four are turnip (quick and cheap), lettuce, tomato (bears every two days) and corn (slow and valuable). The shop sells packs of seeds (packSize) of the first four. Energy (default 60 a day): tilling costs 2, watering 1.
Win: the purse reaches the season's quota before the last day (days) ends; lose when it ends short. The bin may name the quota (props.quota); otherwise it is a share of what an ideal farmer earns on the level's plots with the game's crops and rules, rising with difficulty. Every season starts fresh, with startGold and the starter seeds.
Feel presets: farm-stroll (default), farm-brisk. Feedback table must bind pickup (a harvest) and level_complete (menus sound by themselves); the farm's own events (till, plant, water, harvest, ship, payout, buy, sleep, day_start) are bound by the template.
Scope tiers: small = 2 seasons; medium = 3; large = 4. No enemies, items or bosses: the crops and the economy are the content.
Ask (one at a time, short): How long should a day feel (a relaxed two minutes, or a rush)? How many days in a season, and how many seasons? Which crops (quick cheap ones, slow valuable ones, ones that bear again)? How demanding is the buyer? What is the mood (sunny and gentle, misty and quiet, storybook)?
