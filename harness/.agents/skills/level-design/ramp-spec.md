# Ramp spec

- Level 1: difficulty 0.15-0.3; teaches every verb once; no deaths required to learn.
- Middle levels: +0.1 to +0.15 per level; introduce one new element per level (a new enemy, a new chunk type).
- Last level: 0.6-0.8; combines elements; still fair (the solver must pass, the walker bot should finish).
- The first 10 tiles of every level are hazard-free (enforced by the validator).
